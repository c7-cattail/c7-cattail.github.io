Privacy policy

Effective: November 16, 2026
English translation updated: November 21, 2026
Operator: Catail

This policy applies to Nyaako's Journey (にゃ〜この旅) for iOS, including TestFlight testing, its related website and support service. Game progress stays on your device. Advertising features use Google services.

1. Game progress and settings

The app does not require an account. Cats found, completed pictures, journeys in progress, sound levels and display settings are stored on your device. The app does not send these game records to a Catail server and has no in-game cloud-sync feature.

You can delete local progress and settings through Settings → Delete saved progress. This is separate from deleting support emails.

2. Information received through support

If you email us, we receive your email address, sender name, message and any device details or images you choose to include. We use these to reply, investigate issues, provide necessary information and improve the service.

Please do not send unnecessary personal information, passwords, verification codes or payment details. We use Gmail to receive and store support emails. See Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Website access information

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub Pages records and stores access information such as visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Catail does not add independent analytics tools or advertising cookies to this site.

See the GitHub General Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data collection information. External websites have their own policies.

4. Advertising and privacy choices

The app integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent management. Full-screen ads may appear after a completed journey. Banner ads may appear on screens other than the title screen. Test builds may use test ads.

When advertising is enabled, Google may receive IP addresses, device and app identifiers, ad impressions and interaction information, crash information and other performance or diagnostic data. An approximate location may be inferred from an IP address. This information may be used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and maintain and improve services. Test ads also involve SDK network communication. Catail does not integrate an independent analytics SDK.

The current implementation requests non-personalized ads and disables the publisher first-party ID. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission or location permission. Non-personalized ads still involve communication and processing needed for advertising.

Where required, Google's consent-management tools ask for privacy choices before ads load. When a privacy-choice entry point is required, Ad privacy options appears in Journey settings. You can still play if consent checks or ad loading fail.

For details, see Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services. Processing may vary by region, consent choices and service settings.

5. In-app purchases

A one-time purchase removes banner ads and full-screen ads after journeys. Apple processes purchases and restoration through the App Store and StoreKit. Catail does not receive credit card numbers or other payment details.

The app verifies purchase information obtained from Apple on your device and uses it to remove ads and restore purchases. It does not send purchase information to a Catail-operated server. Deleting game progress does not cancel your purchase. See App Store & Privacy.

6. Retention, security and disclosure

Support information is retained for the scope and duration needed to respond and investigate issues, and deleted when no longer needed. We take steps such as managing account access to protect this information.

Except for the services described in this policy, your consent, legal requirements or similar circumstances, Catail does not provide received support information to third parties. Information managed by Google and GitHub is subject to their retention and deletion policies.

7. Access, correction, deletion and contact

To request access, correction or deletion of information provided through support, contact us below. We may verify that a request is yours where necessary and respond within our capabilities and applicable law.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as features, services or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted here. We will also notify users in the app of significant changes where necessary.
