A little journey with cats

Nyaako's Journey

A world of pictures.
A journey of cats.

100 pictures. 664 cats to meet.

Wander through storybook streets, watercolor gardens, intricate line drawings and colorful patterns. Find cats resting in plain sight or tucked into surprising corners.

Take your time

Tap a cat when you spot it. There is no timer and no penalty for a wrong tap. Gentle music and cheerful meows accompany each discovery. Hints help when you need a little nudge.

Choose your next stop

Browse pictures, search by art style, and revisit any journey. Available for iPhone and iPad, in portrait or landscape.

The game is currently available on the Japanese App Store. An English-language update and wider availability are in preparation.

View on the Japanese App Store

Free to play, with ads and an optional one-time purchase to remove them. All stages and hints are available without a purchase.
