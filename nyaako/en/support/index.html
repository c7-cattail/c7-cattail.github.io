Frequently asked questions

Where can I download the game?

The current release is available on the Japanese App Store. English support and additional countries are being prepared for the next update.

Where is my progress saved?

Your journeys and settings are saved on your device. There is no account registration or in-game cloud sync.

Can I play a completed picture again?

Yes. Start a fresh visit while keeping your previous completion records and total cats found.

How can I reset my progress?

In Settings, choose Delete saved progress, then confirm. This cannot be undone. Your ad-removal purchase is not deleted.

Can I transfer my progress?

The game does not currently have its own progress-transfer or cloud-sync feature.

Can I mute the sound?

Yes. Music, sound effects and meows have separate volume controls.

Can I remove ads?

Choose Settings → Remove ads. A one-time purchase removes both banners and full-screen ads after journeys. The current price is shown in the purchase screen. All stages and hints remain available without a purchase.

How do I restore my purchase?

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase, then choose Settings → Remove ads → Restore purchase. Restoring a purchase is separate from transferring game progress.

How do I change language?

Version 1.1.0 adds Japanese and English. It follows your device language by default, with English for unsupported languages. You can also choose a language in Settings. This update is in preparation.
