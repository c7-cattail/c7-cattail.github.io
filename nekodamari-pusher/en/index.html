Support

Send questions or bug reports to support.catail@gmail.com. Include your phone model, OS, app version and the steps to reproduce the issue if you can.

How do I restore a purchase?

Use the same store account you purchased with, then open Shop and choose Restore Purchases.

How do I get more cats?

Wait for cats on the table to fall, claim a daily gift, or check Missions. At zero cats you can watch an ad for 1,000 cats. If you own Remove Ads, you can get them without watching an ad.

What does resetting do?

Reset Game clears local progress, including your stash, table and album. Your purchases stay available.
