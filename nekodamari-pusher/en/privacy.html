Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Catail handles information in Nekodamari Pusher.

Game progress

Your stash, table, collection, missions and settings are saved on your device. There is no game account. The app does not send this progress to a Catail server.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob and Google's consent management SDK. Google may process approximate location inferred from IP address, device identifiers, ad and app interactions, crash data and performance information for ad delivery, measurement, service improvement and fraud prevention. The game requests non-personalized ads. Where available, you can review ad privacy choices in Settings.

How Google uses information from partner apps · Google Privacy Policy

Purchases

Apple or Google processes payments and keeps purchase records. The app checks the store purchase status to enable purchased features. Catail does not receive card numbers or payment passwords.

Resetting and contacting us

You can erase local progress in Settings. Purchases remain in your store account. If you contact us, Catail uses your email address and message to respond and investigate reported issues. Questions about privacy can be sent to support.catail@gmail.com.
